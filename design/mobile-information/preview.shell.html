Design proposal

The last page on the phone that is still on the old styling

The desktop /information has been rebuilt as a hub: a hero that says what the list is, one search field over everything, and six faces that open a reader — the FAQ, the site map, the guidelines, the two legends, the staff and contact routing, and the API. The phone still shows the page it showed before that: an editor list, two legends written out as sentences, and the guidelines in a scroll box inside a scrolling page. It is missing four of the six blocks entirely.

These are three ways to put the same content on a 390px screen. All three carry everything the desktop page has and nothing it does not; what differs is where the long reads live — behind a door, on a shelf, or printed in one document.

Constraint
Everything the desktop page has, nothing it doesn't.
Reused
css/ull-v2.css and css/pages/mobile-v2.css as they ship: pill, chip, eyebrow, card, button, definition list, sheet, tab bar.
New
A reading kit for the phone — the prose, legends, endpoint and field lists the reader needs — shared by all three.
Same content
One copy of every block, stamped into all three templates by the build.
