Upcoming Levels List

Upcoming Levels List (ULL) is a community-maintained catalogue of upcoming Top 1–100 Extreme Demons in Geometry Dash, projected to place on the Demonlist once they are verified and published. It forecasts the future of the Demonlist and also catalogues worthy unrated Extreme Demons.

Every entry records the level’s creators, its verifier, how far decoration has progressed, the best records and runs players have set on it, and the position the staff team projects it will take.

The three list tiers

Every level on the Upcoming Levels List is positioned on one consistent scale. The three tiers differ only in how strict their inclusion threshold is, forming a hierarchy of probability and quality.

  • All Levels — the full catalogue, with the lowest inclusion threshold: every Geometry Dash Extreme Demon in development with a conceivable chance of being verified and published.
  • Main List — levels that already meet the fundamental standards required to be considered for an official rating by the developer.
  • Future List — the strictest tier: levels with a very high likelihood of imminent verification and publication.

Beyond the lists

  • Upcoming Levels — unverified levels ranked by how close they actually are to a verification.
  • Pending List — levels submitted and awaiting a placement decision from the staff team.
  • Leaderboard — players ranked by the verifications, records and runs they hold on listed levels.
  • Events — the current Level of the Month and the level Closest to Verification.
  • Information — the full guidelines: record acceptance, proof requirements, list procedures and staff duties.

The list is maintained by the ULL staff team and updated continuously. It is not affiliated with RobTop Games.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Upcoming Levels List?
The Upcoming Levels List (ULL) is a community-maintained catalogue of upcoming Top 1–100 Extreme Demons in Geometry Dash — levels that are still in development, decoration or verification and are projected to place on the Demonlist once they are released. It also includes worthy unrated Extreme Demons that would have qualified for a rating at the time they were made.
What are upcoming levels in Geometry Dash?
Upcoming levels are Geometry Dash levels that have been announced or are visibly in development but have not been verified and published yet. In the Extreme Demon community the term usually refers to future Top 1–100 Demonlist contenders: levels still being built, decorated, or attempted by a verifier. The Upcoming Levels List tracks these levels with their creators, verifier, decoration progress, best records and projected placement.
How do I find out which Extreme Demon is coming out next?
The Future List and the Upcoming Levels page are the two places to look. The Future List holds the levels judged most likely to be verified and published soon, while Upcoming Levels ranks every unverified level by how far players have actually progressed on it, so the levels at the top are the closest to a verification.
What is the difference between the All Levels, Main List and Future List?
All three use the same positions; only the inclusion threshold changes. All Levels is the complete catalogue of everything with a conceivable chance of release, the Main List keeps only levels that meet the standards required to be considered for an official rate, and the Future List keeps only levels with a very high likelihood of imminent verification.
How are levels placed on the Upcoming Levels List?
Placements are decided by the list staff — List Moderators, Elder List Moderators, Admins and the List Leader — following the published guidelines. Staff assess a level’s difficulty relative to the levels already on the list, its quality and its likelihood of being verified, then vote on a position. Levels awaiting a decision sit on the Pending List.
How is the ULL leaderboard calculated?
Players earn points for progress on listed levels. A verification is worth twice a full record, completing a level while it is still a layout is worth 1.6 times a record, and ordinary records and runs are scored from the percentage reached and the level’s position in All Levels. A player’s total is the sum of every entry they hold.
Is the Upcoming Levels List affiliated with the Demonlist or RobTop Games?
No. ULL is an independent, community-run project and is not affiliated with RobTop Games. Its guidelines are adapted from, and credit, the Global Demonlist Guidelines.
Is the Upcoming Levels List data available as an API?
Yes. The list is served by a free public JSON API at https://d1-wrkr.ullteam.workers.dev with no authentication required — /api/list returns every level in rank order, and there are endpoints for the Main List, Future List, Pending List, staff and recent changes.
